# Advanced Practice Provider Critical Care Exam Specifications

---

   - C. Subarachnoid hemorrhage and aneurysm
   - D. Subdural hemorrhage
   - E. Epidural hemorrhage

2. Determine diagnosis and management of encephalopathy
   - A. Anoxic/hypoxic brain injury
   - B. Metabolic encephalopathy
   - C. Toxin-induced encephalopathy
   - D. ~~Delirium~~

1. ~~Management of seizures and status epilepticus~~

2. Recognize the diagnosis and management of other neurologic diseases and disorders (e.g., increased intracranial pressure (ICP), neuromuscular disease)

---

## Domain II: Cardiovascular System & Shock (17%)

### Cardiovascular System

1. Determine the diagnosis and management of acute coronary syndromes (ACS)

2. Determine the diagnosis and management of arrhythmias
   - A. Tachyarrhythmias (e.g., Atrial fibrillation/flutter, VT)
   - B. Bradyarrhythmias (e.g., heart blocks, junctional)

3. Determine the diagnosis and management of heart failure / cardiomyopathies
   - A. Left sided (e.g., systolic, diastolic)
   - B. Right sided (e.g., pulmonary hypertension)
   - C. Valvular heart disease

4. Describe indications for invasive and non-invasive hemodynamic monitoring

5. Analyze results from invasive and non-invasive hemodynamic monitoring

6. Manage hypertensive emergency and urgency

7. Determine the diagnosis and management of pericardial disease

8. Recognize indications for and complications of mechanical circulatory support (e.g., intra-aortic balloon pump (IABP), extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO), ventricular assist devices (VADs))

### Shock

9. Determine types of shock
   - A. Distributive
   - B. Obstructive
   - C. Hypovolemic
   - D. Cardiogenic

10. Describe the pathophysiology behind types of shock

11. Manage types of shock
    - A. Resuscitation strategy
    - B. Pharmacological

---

## Domain III: Pulmonary System (17%)

1. Determine the causes, types, and management of respiratory failure
   - A. Hypoxemic
   - B. Hypercapnic

2. Manage respiratory/ventilatory support
   - A. Initiation and maintenance of invasive and non-invasive mechanical ventilation
     - i. Airway management (e.g., endotracheal intubation, tracheostomy)
     - ii. Modes
     - iii. Oxygenation
     - iv. Ventilation (CO2)
     - v. Respiratory system compliance (lung mechanics)
   - B. Complications of invasive and non-invasive mechanical ventilation
   - C. Liberation from respiratory/ventilatory support
   - D. Other oxygen delivery devices (e.g., heated high-flow nasal cannula)

3. Determine the diagnosis and management of pulmonary diseases/disorders
   - A. Airway
     - i. Asthma
     - ii. Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD)
     - iii. Upper airway obstruction
   - B. Parenchymal
     - i. Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS)
     - ii. Pneumonia
     - iii. Pulmonary edema
     - iv. Interstitial lung disease
     - v. Atelectasis
   - C. Pleural
     - i. Pleural effusions
     - ii. Pneumothorax
   - D. Other/Vascular
     - i. Hemoptysis
     - ii. Pulmonary embolism (PE)
     - iii. Traumatic, iatrogenic, and environmental lung injury

---

## Domain IV: Gastrointestinal System (7%)

1. Determine the diagnosis and management of gastrointestinal (GI) disorders
   - A. GI bleed
   - B. Acute abdomen (e.g., gallbladder and biliary tract disorders, pancreatitis, perforations)
   - C. Liver Failure
     - i. Acute hepatitis / fulminant hepatic failure
     - ii. Decompensated cirrhosis
     - iii. Portal hypertension
     - iv. Hepatorenal syndrome

---

## Domain V: Renal, Endocrine & Metabolic Systems (12%)

1. Determine diagnosis and management of acid base disorders
   - A. Respiratory
   - B. Metabolic
   - C. Mixed

2. Determine diagnosis and management of acute kidney injury
   - A. Acute kidney injury
     - i. Pre-renal
     - ii. Intrinsic
     - iii. Post-renal
   - B. Renal replacement therapy

3. Manage electrolyte imbalances (e.g., sodium-water balance, potassium disorders, hypercalcemia, hypomagnesemia)

4. Determine diagnosis and management of diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) and hyperglycemic hyperosmolar non-ketotic coma (HONK)

5. Recognize diagnosis and management of thyroid emergencies

6. Recognize diagnosis and management of adrenal insufficiency

---

## Domain VI: Hematologic & Oncologic Disorders (5%)

1. Recognize the diagnosis and management of platelet disorders

2. Recognize the diagnosis and management of coagulopathies and hypercoagulable states

3. Recognize the diagnosis and management of hematologic and oncologic emergencies (e.g., acute leukemia, tumor lysis syndrome (TLS))

---

## Domain VII: Infectious Disease (7%)

1. Determine the diagnosis and management of systemic infections and sepsis/septic shock
   - A. CNS (e.g., encephalitis, meningitis)
   - B. Cardiovascular infections (e.g., endocarditis, pericarditis)
   - C. Pulmonary
   - D. Gastrointestinal and intra-abdominal (e.g., colitis, gallbladder and biliary, peritonitis)
   - E. Genitourinary
   - F. Skin and soft tissue (e.g., necrotizing fasciitis)
   - G. Bacteremia

2. Recognize special considerations related to infectious diseases
   - A. Immunocompromised states (e.g., opportunistic infections)
   - B. Catheter and device related infections
   - C. Resistant organisms

---

## Domain VIII: Diagnostics & Procedures (5%)

1. Evaluate indications for critical care procedures and diagnostics

2. Assess for complications from procedures

3. Manage complications from procedures

4. Integrate point-of-care ultrasounds (POCUS) for respiratory failure and shock states
   - A. Pulmonary (e.g., A-line/B-line, pleural effusion, pneumothorax)
   - B. Cardiac (e.g., global cardiac function, IVC collapsibility, pericardial effusion)
   - C. Abdominal (e.g., bladder pressure, free fluid, intra-abdominal pressure)
   - D. Vascular imaging
   - E. Procedural use/guidance

---

## Domain IX: Special Considerations: Surgical, Trauma & Obstetrics (7%)

1. Assess for post-operative complications (e.g., bleeding, complex wound care, drain management, infections)

2. Recognize complications of trauma (e.g., burns, compartment syndromes, inhalation injury, near drowning, rhabdomyolysis, rib fractures, TBIs)

3. Recognize obstetrics emergencies (e.g., peri- and postpartum) and related special considerations (e.g., airway management, acid-base disorders, pharmacology)

---

## Domain X: Pharmacology & Toxicology (8%)

1. Determine indications for and management of analgesia, sedation, and paralytics

2. Recognize dynamic management strategies for antimicrobial therapies (e.g., allergic reactions, non-allergic toxicity)

3. Manage toxicology and poisoning (e.g., Acetaminophen, beta blockers, calcium channel blockers, tricyclics)

4. Manage drug overdose and withdrawal (e.g., alcohols, benzodiazepine, opiates)

---

## Domain XI: Patient-Centered Care, Health Equity & Ethics (7%)

### Patient-Centered Care

1. Integrate team-based care

2. Determine aspects of goals of care

3. Evaluate if patients are appropriate for palliative care

4. Demonstrate patient-centered communication strategies

5. Implement prevention strategies to avoid hospital acquired complications and infections (e.g., bundles, standards of care, value-based care / "Choosing Wisely" campaign)

### Health Equity

6. Assess at risk populations and communities made vulnerable and their barriers to healthcare, including cost, lack of accessibility, and lack of equitable resources

7. Demonstrate culturally responsive care, recognizing and respecting patients' identities

8. Recognize the impact of social determinants of health on patient outcomes

9. Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams to lead and implement efforts to protect the population health of the most vulnerable populations

10. Identify personal, patient, and inter-professional biases (e.g., cultural bias, implicit bias, microaggressions, stereotypes)

### Ethics

11. Apply the key components of patients' rights (e.g., informed consent, care for incarcerated and incapacitated patients, medical decision making, patient autonomy)

12. Apply the key components of workplace safety

13. Identify protocol(s) and contra-indications related to brain death

14. Recognize situations that may lead to potentially non-beneficial care

15. Incorporate processes that support ethical conduct in practice and scholarship
