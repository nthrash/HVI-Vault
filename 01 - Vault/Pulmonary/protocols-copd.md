# Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)

---

## 1. Overview

COPD is a progressive lung disease characterized by persistent respiratory symptoms and airflow limitation due to airway and/or alveolar abnormalities, usually caused by significant exposure to noxious particles or gases. It affects approximately 16 million people in the US and is the third leading cause of death globally.

**Pathophysiology:** Chronic airway inflammation → structural changes → small airway narrowing + lung parenchymal destruction → airflow limitation + impaired gas exchange.

---

## 2. Diagnosis

**Gold Standard:** Post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC **<70%** on spirometry (GOLD criteria)

| Test | Finding |
|------|---------|
| Spirometry | FEV1/FVC <70% post-bronchodilator |
| Chest X-ray | Hyperinflation, flattened diaphragm |
| CT chest | Emphysema, air trapping |
| Alpha-1 antitrypsin | Consider in younger patients or non-smokers |

---

## 3. Severity Stratification (GOLD)

| Grade | FEV1 % Predicted |
|-------|-----------------|
| GOLD 1 (Mild) | ≥80% |
| GOLD 2 (Moderate) | 50–79% |
| GOLD 3 (Severe) | 30–49% |
| GOLD 4 (Very Severe) | <30% |

## 4. Acute Exacerbation Management

### Bronchodilators

| Agent | Dose | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| **Albuterol (SABA)** | 2.5 mg neb q4–6h PRN | 90–180 mcg inhaler q4–6h PRN |
| **Ipratropium (SAMA)** | 500 mcg neb q6–8h | 2 puffs (34 mcg) q6h PRN |
| **Duoneb** (albuterol/ipratropium 2.5/0.5 mg) | 1 vial q1–2h (up to 3 doses), then q2–4h PRN | Max 6 vials in first 12h for acute exacerbation |
| **Combivent Respimat** | 1 inhalation q6h | Combo MDI via Respimat device |

### Systemic Corticosteroids

| Setting | Agent | Dose |
|---------|-------|------|
| Severe / intubation concern | Methylprednisolone | 125 mg IV daily (or 40–80 mg IV q12h) |
| Moderate exacerbation | Methylprednisolone | 40–80 mg IV q12–24h |
| Step-down / oral | Prednisone | 40–50 mg daily × 5 days |

> *Note: 5-day course is generally sufficient for most exacerbations. REDUCE trial: no benefit to longer courses.*

### Antibiotics

**Indications:** Purulent sputum, increased sputum volume, or increased dyspnea (≥2 of 3 Anthonisen criteria), or requiring mechanical ventilation.

| Scenario | Antibiotic |
|----------|-----------|
| Outpatient / mild | Azithromycin 500 mg day 1, then 250 mg days 2–5 **OR** Doxycycline 100 mg BID × 5–7 days |
| Inpatient, no Pseudomonas risk | Ceftriaxone 1–2 g IV daily **OR** Levofloxacin 500 mg IV/PO **OR** Moxifloxacin 400 mg IV/PO |
| Pseudomonas risk factors present | Piperacillin-tazobactam 4.5 g IV q6h **OR** Cefepime 2 g IV q8h **OR** Ceftazidime 2 g IV q8h |
| Influenza suspected | Oseltamivir 75 mg PO BID **OR** Peramivir 600 mg IV × 1 |

*Pseudomonas risk factors: prior Pseudomonas isolation, structural lung disease, recent hospitalization, recent antibiotics, severe COPD (FEV1 <30%).*

## 5. Non-Invasive Ventilation (BiPAP)

### Indications
- RR >30/min with significant respiratory distress
- Hypercapnic encephalopathy from COPD exacerbation
- pH <7.35 with PaCO₂ >45 mmHg

### Contraindications
- Need for immediate intubation
- Vomiting or high aspiration risk
- Copious secretions / inability to manage airway

### BiPAP Settings

| Parameter | Starting | Titration Target |
|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| IPAP | 10 cmH₂O | Up to ~18 cmH₂O |
| EPAP | 5 cmH₂O | 5–8 cmH₂O |
| Driving pressure (IPAP−EPAP) | 5 cmH₂O | Titrate for adequate TV |

- Elevated EPAP (~8 cmH₂O) can counteract autoPEEP and reduce work of breathing
- Target tidal volume ≥400 mL; minute ventilation ≥5–6 L/min
- If TV <300–400 mL: widen driving pressure; maximum ~20/5 cmH₂O
- If BiPAP contraindicated: consider HFNC as alternative

## 6. Mechanical Ventilation in COPD

**Primary concern: AutoPEEP (gas trapping)**

AutoPEEP occurs when expiratory flow does not return to zero before the next breath → gas trapping → ↑ intrathoracic pressure → ↓ venous return → hypotension + ventilator dyssynchrony.

### Suggested Starting Settings

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Mode | Volume-cycled (AC/VC) |
| Tidal volume | 8 mL/kg IBW |
| Respiratory rate | ~14 breaths/min |
| PEEP | 5–8 cmH₂O |
| I:E ratio | Extend expiratory time (1:3 or 1:4) |

### AutoPEEP Management

| Problem | Intervention |
|---------|-------------|
| Detected autoPEEP | ↓ RR first (most effective) |
| Over-breathing vent | Sedation/opioid to suppress drive |
| Triggering difficulty | Increase set PEEP slightly toward intrinsic PEEP level |
| Inadequate exhalation time | ↓ Inspiratory time; ↑ I:E ratio |

*Diagnosis: Persistent expiratory flow at end-exhalation (flow never reaches zero).*

## 7. Maintenance Inhalers (Non-Acute)

| Class | Agent | Dose |
|-------|-------|------|
| LABA | Salmeterol | 50 mcg BID |
| LABA | Formoterol | 12 mcg BID or 20 mcg neb BID |
| LABA | Arformoterol (Brovana) | 15 mcg neb BID |
| LAMA | Tiotropium | 18 mcg daily |
| LAMA | Umeclidinium | 62.5 mcg daily |
| LABA/ICS | Salmeterol/Fluticasone | 50/250 mcg BID |
| LABA/LAMA | Indacaterol/Glycopyrronium | 27.5/15.6 mcg daily |
| LABA/LAMA | Vilanterol/Umeclidinium | 25/62.5 mcg daily |
| Triple (LABA/LAMA/ICS) | Fluticasone/Umeclidinium/Vilanterol | 100/62.5/25 mcg daily |
| PDE-4 inhibitor | Roflumilast | 500 mcg daily (chronic bronchitis + severe COPD) |

*ICS: Use in combination with LABA for frequent exacerbators; not indicated as monotherapy in COPD.*
*Budesonide neb: Typically not used in acute exacerbation; preferred for mild cases or steroid contraindication.*

## 8. Key Trials

| Trial | Finding |
|-------|---------|
| **UPLIFT** | Tiotropium improved lung function, reduced exacerbations, improved QoL over 4 years |
| **TORCH** | Salmeterol/fluticasone reduced exacerbations and improved health status; did **not** significantly reduce mortality (p=0.052) |
| **FLAME** | Indacaterol/glycopyrronium (LABA/LAMA) more effective than salmeterol/fluticasone (LABA/ICS) in reducing exacerbations |
| **INSPIRE** | Both LABA/ICS and tiotropium effective for exacerbation prevention |

---

## 9. TLDR — Acute Exacerbation

- Duonebs q1–2h, IV methylprednisolone 40–125 mg
- Antibiotics: ceftriaxone + azithromycin (no Pseudomonas risk); pip-tazo or cefepime if Pseudomonas risk
- Add vancomycin until MRSA PCR returns if gram-positive coverage needed
- BiPAP if hypercapnic or significant distress; RSI if failing
- Once stable: start Brovana + Pulmicort + Duonebs; continue IV solumedrol 40 mg q12h
