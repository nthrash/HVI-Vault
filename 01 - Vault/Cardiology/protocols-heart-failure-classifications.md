# Heart Failure Classifications

Comprehensive heart failure classifications and etiologies for clinical reference.

---

## By Ejection Fraction

| Category | Definition | Typical Features | EF |
|:---------|:-----------|:-----------------|:---|
| HFrEF | Systolic dysfunction | LV dilation/remodeling; reduced contractility | ≤40% |
| HFmrEF | Mildly reduced EF | Mixed systolic/diastolic features | 41–49% |
| HFpEF | Preserved EF with elevated filling pressures | Diastolic dysfunction; concentric LVH common | ≥50% |
| HFimpEF | Improved EF from prior ≤40% | EF increases to >40% on therapy; continue GDMT | Prior ≤40% → >40% |

|:-------|:---------|:-----------|

## By Hemodynamic/Structural Phenotype

| Phenotype | Definition | Common Contexts | Typical EF |
|:----------|:-----------|:----------------|:-----------|
| Left-sided HF | LV failure → pulmonary congestion | CAD, HTN, valvular disease, cardiomyopathies | Any |
| Right-sided HF | RV failure → systemic venous congestion | Left HF, pulmonary HTN, RV infarct | Any |
| High-output HF | CO normal/high but inadequate for demand | Severe anemia, thyrotoxicosis, AV fistula, beriberi, cirrhosis | Often preserved |
| Low-output HF | Reduced cardiac output | Advanced HFrEF, severe valvular stenosis | Reduced |
| Acute HF | Sudden decompensation | ADHF, flash pulmonary edema, cardiogenic shock | Any |
| Chronic HF | Long-standing syndrome | Stable outpatient HF | Any |
| De novo HF | First presentation of HF | Myocarditis, MI, new valvular lesion | Any |
| Acute-on-chronic HF | Exacerbation of known HF | Diet/med nonadherence, arrhythmia, infection | Baseline EF |

## By Bedside Congestion/Perfusion Profile

| Profile | Perfusion | Congestion | Typical Management Focus |
|:--------|:----------|:-----------|:-------------------------|
| Warm–dry | Adequate | No | Optimize GDMT |
| Warm–wet | Adequate | Yes | Diuretics, vasodilators, afterload reduction |
| Cold–wet | Poor | Yes | Diuresis plus inotropes/vasodilators; consider advanced therapies |
| Cold–dry | Poor | No or unclear | Gentle fluids if preload-depleted; inotropes/vasoconstrictors as needed |

## Ischemic vs Nonischemic Overview

| Group | Subtype/Examples | Key Features | Typical EF |
|:------|:-----------------|:-------------|:-----------|
| Ischemic cardiomyopathy | Prior MI, multivessel CAD, ischemic scar | Regional wall motion abnormalities; LV remodeling | Often reduced |
| Nonischemic cardiomyopathy | Dilated, hypertrophic, restrictive, arrhythmogenic, noncompaction; inflammatory; toxin; endocrine; infiltrative; genetic | Global or noncoronary patterns; family history may be present | Any (subtype-dependent) |

---

## Dilated Cardiomyopathy (DCM)

| Etiology | Examples | Clues | Typical EF |
|:---------|:---------|:------|:-----------|
| Idiopathic | — | Exclusion of secondary causes; family history | Reduced |
| Genetic | TTN, LMNA, DES, DSP, DMD, MYH7 | Conduction disease, arrhythmias, family SCD | Reduced |
| Inflammatory | Viral myocarditis, autoimmune, giant cell | Recent viral prodrome; MRI edema; troponin elevation | Reduced to variable |
| Peripartum | Late pregnancy to 5 months postpartum | Preeclampsia, multiparity; higher risk in Black women | Reduced |
| Tachycardia-mediated | AF with RVR, incessant SVT | Improves after rate/rhythm control | Reduced, reversible |
| Toxin (alcohol/drugs) | Alcohol, cocaine, methamphetamine | Substance history; abstinence improves alcohol DCM | Reduced |
| Chemotherapy | Anthracyclines, trastuzumab, TKIs, checkpoint inhibitors | Temporal link; strain drop precedes EF decline | Reduced |
| Nutritional/metabolic | Thiamine, selenium deficiency | Malnutrition, bariatric surgery, alcoholism | Reduced |
| Endocrine | Hypo-/hyperthyroid, pheochromocytoma, acromegaly | Thyroid abnormality; catecholamine surge | Reduced to variable |
| Stress-induced | Takotsubo cardiomyopathy | Apical ballooning; emotional/physical stress; recovery | Reduced, reversible |
| Infectious (nonviral) | Chagas, Lyme | Geography/travel; conduction disease (Chagas) | Reduced |

## Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (HCM) and Hypertensive Phenotype

| Etiology | Examples | Clues | Typical EF |
|:---------|:---------|:------|:-----------|
| Sarcomeric HCM | MYH7, MYBPC3, TNNT2 | Asymmetric septal hypertrophy; LVOT obstruction | Preserved (HFpEF); reduced in end stage |
| Hypertensive heart disease | Longstanding hypertension | Concentric LVH, diastolic dysfunction | Preserved |

---

## Restrictive Cardiomyopathy (RCM) and Mimics

| Etiology | Examples | Clues | Typical EF |
|:---------|:---------|:------|:-----------|
| Amyloidosis | ATTR (wild-type, variant), AL | Thick walls with low ECG voltage; apical sparing on GLS | Preserved → reduced later |
| Sarcoidosis | Cardiac sarcoid | AV block, VT; patchy LGE on MRI; FDG-PET uptake | Variable |
| Hemochromatosis | Iron overload | High ferritin/TSAT; T2* MRI; liver involvement | Preserved → reduced |
| Storage diseases | Fabry, Danon, Pompe | Multisystem signs; alpha-gal A deficiency (Fabry) | Preserved → reduced |
| Endomyocardial disease | Hypereosinophilic, endomyocardial fibrosis | Restrictive filling; apical thrombus/fibrosis | Preserved |
| Radiation-induced | Post mediastinal radiation | Pericardial calcification; valvular disease | Preserved → reduced |
| Constrictive pericarditis (mimic) | TB, surgery, radiation | Discordant LV/RV pressures; septal bounce | Preserved (myocardium normal) |

---

## Arrhythmogenic and Structural Cardiomyopathies

| Etiology | Examples | Clues | Typical EF |
|:---------|:---------|:------|:-----------|
| Arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy | ARVC/arrhythmogenic LV | VT with LBBB morphology; epsilon waves; desmosomal variants | RV ± LV reduced |
| LV noncompaction | Developmental trabeculation | Prominent trabeculae; MRI/echo ratio criteria | Variable |

---

## Valvular, Pericardial, and Great Vessel

| Category | Examples | Clues | Typical EF |
|:---------|:---------|:------|:-----------|
| Valvular stenosis | Aortic stenosis, mitral stenosis | Murmurs; pressure overload; echo gradient | Preserved early → reduced late |
| Valvular regurgitation | MR, AR, TR | Volume overload; chamber dilation | Preserved early → reduced late |
| Mixed/multivalve | Combined lesions | Post-radiation, rheumatic, degenerative | Variable |
| Pericardial disease | Constrictive, effusive-constrictive, tamponade physiology | Kussmaul sign; septal bounce; calcification | Preserved (myocardium) |

---

## Right Heart and Pulmonary Vascular Disease

| Etiology | Examples | Clues | Typical EF |
|:---------|:---------|:------|:-----------|
| Pulmonary hypertension | WHO Group 1–5 | Elevated PAP; RV hypertrophy/dilation | RV failure with preserved LV EF |
| Chronic lung disease | COPD, interstitial lung disease | Hypoxic vasoconstriction; cor pulmonale | RV dysfunction |
| Acute RV overload | Massive/submassive PE | Sudden dyspnea; RV strain on echo/CT | RV failure |

---

## Endocrine, Metabolic, and Systemic

| Etiology | Examples | Clues | Typical EF |
|:---------|:---------|:------|:-----------|
| Diabetes/obesity | Obesity cardiomyopathy | LVH, HFpEF; epicardial adiposity | Preserved common |
| Thyroid disorders | Hypo-/hyperthyroidism | Brady/tachyarrhythmias; myopathy | Variable |
| Adrenal | Cushing's, pheochromocytoma | Hypertension; crises | Variable |
| Nutritional | Thiamine (beriberi), selenium | High-output HF; neuropathy | Often preserved/high output |
| Autoimmune/systemic | SLE, scleroderma | Myocarditis/PH; multisystem disease | Variable |
| Infiltrative (systemic) | Amyloid, sarcoid, iron | Multiorgan clues; tissue diagnosis | Variable |

## Toxins and Therapeutics

| Agent | Examples | Clues | Typical EF |
|:------|:---------|:------|:-----------|
| Alcohol | Chronic heavy use | Improves with abstinence | Reduced |
| Illicit drugs | Cocaine, methamphetamine | Vasospasm, myocarditis, DCM | Reduced |
| Chemotherapy | Anthracyclines, trastuzumab, TKIs | Cardio-oncology surveillance; GLS drop precedes EF | Reduced |
| Radiation | Mediastinal radiation | Also valvular/pericardial/coronary disease | Variable |
| Immune therapies | Checkpoint inhibitors | Myocarditis; troponin elevation | Variable |

## Infectious and Inflammatory

| Etiology | Examples | Clues | Typical EF |
|:---------|:---------|:------|:-----------|
| Viral myocarditis | Coxsackie, adenovirus, parvovirus B19 | Chest pain, troponin rise, MRI edema | Reduced to variable |
| Bacterial | Lyme, diphtheria | Conduction disease; exposure history | Variable |
| Parasitic | Chagas (T. cruzi) | Latin America exposure; apical aneurysm | Reduced |
| Autoimmune myocarditis | Giant cell, sarcoid | VT/heart block; biopsy/MRI | Variable |

---

## Congenital and Structural Heart Disease

| Category | Examples | Clues | Typical EF |
|:---------|:---------|:------|:-----------|
| Shunts | ASD, VSD, PDA | Volume overload; Eisenmenger risk if late | Variable |
| Outflow lesions | Coarctation, subaortic stenosis | Upper–lower BP gradient; collateral flow | Variable |
| Post-repair HF | Tetralogy, TGA, Fontan circulation | Arrhythmias; RV/LV failure depending on lesion | Variable |

## Quick Mapping to Common Terms

| Term | Meaning | Typical Examples |
|:-----|:--------|:-----------------|
| Ischemic HF | HF due to CAD/MI | Prior MI, multivessel CAD, ischemic scar |
| Nonischemic HF | HF not due to CAD | Dilated, hypertrophic, restrictive, valvular, endocrine, toxins, myocarditis |
| Restrictive cardiomyopathy | Small, stiff ventricles with diastolic dysfunction | Amyloidosis, hemochromatosis, sarcoidosis, endomyocardial fibrosis |
