# SCIP Protocol — Surgical Care Improvement Project

Quick reference for perioperative care measures in cardiac and vascular surgical patients.

---

## 1. Prophylactic Antibiotics

| Measure | Standard |
|---------|---------|
| **Timing** | Within **60 min** before incision (120 min for vancomycin/fluoroquinolones) |
| **Cardiac surgery** | Cefazolin 2 g IV (3 g if >120 kg); vancomycin if MRSA risk or beta-lactam allergy |
| **Duration** | Discontinue within **24 hours** post-op (48 h for cardiac surgery is acceptable) |
| **Redosing** | Repeat cefazolin if procedure >4 h or EBL >1.5 L |

---

## 2. Glucose Control

| Measure | Target |
|---------|--------|
| **Cardiac surgery patients** | Blood glucose **<180 mg/dL** in the 18–24 h post-op |
| **Preferred agent** | Continuous insulin infusion per protocol |
| **Avoid** | Hypoglycemia (<70 mg/dL) — check q1–2h while on infusion |

---

## 3. Beta-Blocker Management

| Measure | Standard |
|---------|---------|
| **Patients on pre-op beta-blockers** | **Continue perioperatively** — do not hold |
| **New initiation** | Not recommended acutely (POISE trial: ↑ stroke risk with new periop BB) |
| **Resumption post-op** | Resume as soon as hemodynamically appropriate |

---

## 4. VTE Prophylaxis

| Measure | Standard |
|---------|---------|
| **Timing** | Begin within **24 hours** post-op (or as soon as bleeding risk allows) |
| **Cardiac / major vascular** | Sequential compression devices (SCDs) intraoperatively + pharmacologic when appropriate |
| **Pharmacologic options** | UFH 5,000 units SQ q8h or enoxaparin 40 mg SQ daily |
| **High bleeding risk** | Mechanical prophylaxis only until safe to initiate pharmacologic |

---

## 5. Hair Removal

| Measure | Standard |
|---------|---------|
| **Method** | Clippers only — **no razors** (↑ SSI risk with razors) |
| **Timing** | Immediately before procedure |

---

## 6. Normothermia

| Measure | Standard |
|---------|---------|
| **Target** | Maintain temperature **≥36°C** perioperatively |
| **Measures** | Warming blankets, warmed IV fluids, forced-air warming |
| **Hypothermia risk** | ↑ SSI, coagulopathy, cardiac arrhythmias |

---

## 7. Urinary Catheter Removal

| Measure | Standard |
|---------|---------|
| **Remove by** | Postoperative day 1 or 2 when clinically feasible |
| **Exception** | Prolonged procedure, hemodynamic instability, urologic indication |
| **CAUTI prevention** | Ensure indication documented daily while catheter in place |

---

## 8. Special Populations: LVAD & Transplant Caveats

### LVAD

| Measure | Caveat |
|---------|--------|
| **Antibiotics** | Antibiotic prophylaxis extends beyond standard SCIP duration for LVAD implant procedures — typically per institutional protocol (often 48 h); driveline site infection is a major complication driver |
| **VTE prophylaxis** | LVAD patients require **therapeutic anticoagulation** (warfarin ± aspirin) post-implant — standard SQ heparin VTE prophylaxis is replaced by therapeutic anticoagulation per device protocol; confirm with surgery and device team before initiating any anticoagulation |
| **Glucose control** | Same target <180 mg/dL; tight glycemic control especially important given infectious risk at driveline site |
| **Beta-blockers** | Continue if on pre-op; device-supported patients may tolerate beta-blockade better hemodynamically |

### Heart Transplant

| Measure | Caveat |
|---------|--------|
| **Antibiotics** | Prophylaxis regimen often institution-specific and extended; includes antifungal prophylaxis (fluconazole) and PCP prophylaxis (TMP-SMX) post-transplant — beyond standard SCIP antibiotic window |
| **Glucose control** | Calcineurin inhibitors (tacrolimus, cyclosporine) and corticosteroids cause significant **post-transplant hyperglycemia** — glucose targets may need tighter management; insulin infusion often required for extended period |
| **Beta-blockers** | Heart is **denervated** post-transplant — beta-blocker response is altered; HR is typically higher at rest; chronotropic response to exercise blunted; discuss any beta-blocker use with transplant team |
| **VTE prophylaxis** | Standard pharmacologic prophylaxis applies when bleeding risk acceptable; confirm timing with surgical team given coagulopathy risk in early post-transplant period |

### Lung Transplant

| Measure | Caveat |
|---------|--------|
| **Antibiotics** | Extended prophylaxis per transplant protocol, including anti-fungal and CMV prophylaxis — not covered under standard SCIP antibiotic measures |
| **Glucose control** | Same steroid/calcineurin-related hyperglycemia concern as heart transplant; aggressive insulin protocol typically warranted |
| **VTE prophylaxis** | Standard principles apply; timing and agent selection per surgical team given anastomotic healing concerns |
| **Normothermia** | Especially important — hypothermia impairs early graft function in lung transplant recipients |

---

## 9. Quick SCIP Checklist

- [ ] Prophylactic antibiotic given within 60 min of incision
- [ ] Antibiotic discontinued within 24–48 h post-op
- [ ] Blood glucose <180 mg/dL in first 18–24 h (cardiac surgery)
- [ ] Pre-op beta-blocker continued
- [ ] VTE prophylaxis initiated within 24 h post-op
- [ ] Clippers used (no razor) for hair removal
- [ ] Patient normothermic perioperatively
- [ ] Foley removed by POD 1–2
