## What Is Code Gray?

Code Gray recognizes that one or more Memorial Hermann entities are no longer operating in a normal day-to-day environment due to internal or external impacts affecting the ability to maintain a normal staffing model (e.g. hurricane, mass casualty incident, severe weather, building fire). Under this operating status, MH-owned or -operated facilities have greater flexibility with staffing models, pay practices, and general operations.

- The **Incident Command** structure will be fully set up and activated — in person, virtual, or hybrid, as defined by the incident commander.
- Code Gray may be utilized for a variety of disasters or planned events that may impact normal staffing.

### Code Gray vs. Internal Disaster

- An **Internal Disaster** recognizes impacts within a facility that affect operations (e.g. busted pipe, HVAC failure) but do **not** affect the staffing model. Staffing cannot be the primary justification for calling an Internal Disaster.
- A facility can be on both a Code Gray and Internal Disaster, but neither requires the other.
- Internal Disasters are shared with external agencies (RAC/EMS) to communicate operational status.

### System vs. Individual Code Gray

- Code Gray can be called individually at any MH-owned or -operated facility or system-wide for events such as hurricanes, winter storms, severe weather, or patient surge impacting all system operations.
- Due to geographic differences, a grouping of locations may be affected while others are not (e.g. May 2024 Derecho).
- When a system-wide Code Gray is lifted, each individual site has the option to lift the code when it is safe to do so.

### What Code Gray Provides

- **Incident Command** — a defined command structure for streamlined communication and decision making
- **Communication & Marketing** — resources to align internal and external messaging
- **Disaster Pay Practices** — see allHR > My Money > Special Pay Practices
- **Disaster Cost Center** — set up and provided by Finance to respective leaders
- **Staff Support Services** — access to EAP, Food & Nutritional Support, Chaplain Services, Diversional Activities, Employee Health Services, etc.

## Disaster Team Designations

All Memorial Hermann employees are deemed essential and assigned to one of three response teams. Department leadership reviews and designates team members. All employees must complete and review their designation in **Workday** via the Disaster Planning tool.

| Team | Role | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Team A** | Ride Out Team | Response team responsible for being at the care delivery site throughout the period of impact |
| **Team B** | Preparation/Recovery Team | Prepares the facility before Team A arrives and relieves Team A once it is safe to do so |
| **Team CS** | Central Staffing | Picks up shifts to fill staffing gaps as needed; expected to be flexible |

## Staffing Models Under Code Gray

### Emergency Staffing

- Allows leaders to hold staff at the facility until the replacement arrives (1:1 swap)
- Leaders may also ask staff to voluntarily come in ahead of severe weather
- Can be utilized **with or without notice**

### Disaster Staffing

- Requires **36-hour notice** of a disaster
- Utilizes the ride-out team structure (Team A, Team B, Team CS) as identified in the Workday Disaster Plan tool

---

## Disaster Staffing Timeline

### Team B — Preparation & Readiness (36+ to 12 hours before impact)

- Prepares the facility for imminent severe weather or disaster
- Ensures additional food, medical supplies, and other essential resources are in place
- Sets up staff sleeping accommodations
- Released once Team A members arrive at the care delivery site

### Team A — Response (12 hours before impact until safe to travel)

- Secure personal property and family during the preparation phase
- Report to the facility at the designated time to maintain operations throughout the disaster
- Remain on-site until relieved by Team B

### Team B — Recovery (after the disaster)

- Returns as soon as safely possible to assist in recovery efforts
- If the care delivery site can return to normal staffing rotations, they may do so without utilizing Team B

### Team CS — As Needed

- Picks up shifts to fill staffing gaps throughout the event

## Exemptions & Scheduling Adjustments

- Employees on approved and scheduled PTO (two or more consecutive days) at least **two weeks in advance** are excused if the time off coincides with a required report date.
- All team members must be available to work regardless of standard work schedule.
- Work schedules may fluctuate during recovery; the goal is to restore normal operations as quickly as possible.

### Team A Exemption Criteria

Employees may request exemption from Team A under these circumstances:

- Employee provides care for a child under two (2) years of age **or** an elderly immediate relative who cannot self-care, and no other family member can provide this care
- Employee provides care that cannot otherwise be delivered for an immediate relative who is disabled or has chronic illness
- Employee has a child under two years of age and both the employee and the child's other parent work for an emergency services employer (e.g. nursing, other hospital, law enforcement, fire/rescue) with simultaneous schedules
- Employee has a child under two years of age and both parents work for Memorial Hermann (one parent may be exempt)

> If an employee meets exemption criteria for Team A, they will automatically be placed on **Team B**.

## Responsibilities

### Leader Responsibilities

- Ensure all team members understand Code Gray Emergency Staffing and Disaster Staffing
- Review staffing levels in Workday at least **twice a year** for adequate Team A and Team B coverage
- When disaster teams are activated, determine whether full deployment is necessary — if reduced staffing is appropriate, identify the required number and roster accordingly

### Staff Responsibilities

- Understand what Code Gray Emergency Staffing and Disaster Staffing entails
- Review and update your Disaster Planning in Workday **annually**

## Staffing FAQ

**If I'm on Team A, will I always be called in?**
Not necessarily. Leaders use discretion based on need when identifying which staff members are asked to report.

**What if I'm on Team A but my leader doesn't schedule me?**
You may not be needed for every Code Gray event. However, you may be called for recovery efforts or to support the disaster in other ways.

**What if I'm on Team A but unable to make it to work?**
Contact your leader immediately.
