> Memorial Hermann Health System Policy — Last reviewed 06/21/2022 (Version 9)

## Expectations

Employees must follow all departmental call-in procedures and report absences or tardiness in a timely manner. If your department does not have specific procedures, you are required to **call your immediate supervisor at least two hours before the start of your shift**, absent extenuating circumstances.

## Definitions

**Call-in Violation**
Failure to follow prescribed departmental call-in procedures or the two-hour rule.

**No-Call/No-Show (NCNS)**
Not calling in at any time before the start of your shift **and** not arriving to work at any time during your shift, absent extenuating circumstances.

**Job Abandonment**
Two consecutive NCNS incidents — considered a **voluntary separation**.

## Disciplinary Action

All disciplinary actions for violations of this policy must be entered into **Workday**.

| Violation | Recommended Action |
|---|---|
| **First call-in violation** | Verbal Warning |
| **Call-in violation with prior disciplinary action** | Progressive disciplinary action |
| **First NCNS** | Final Written Warning |
| **NCNS with prior Final Written Warning** | Separation of employment |
| **NCNS within first 90 days of employment** | Separation of employment |

### Late Arrival Without Calling In

If you do not call in before the start of your shift but arrive late, you are subject to discipline under the **Attendance and Punctuality Policy** (not this policy). Only one disciplinary action will result from any single incident — whichever policy results in the higher level of discipline applies.
