## Admission Workflow

### 1. Receive the Admission

- Get report from the transferring team (ED, cath lab, floor, OSH, OR)
- Identify the admitting cardiologist and whether surgery is involved

### 2. Notify All Three Teams

Per the [call tree](onboarding-call-tree.md), **all ICU admissions** require notification to:

| Team | Contact |
| --- | --- |
| PCCM Fellow | Phone# 49749 |
| On-Call Cardiologist | On-call schedule |
| On-Call Surgeon | On-call schedule (if MCS present or anticipated) |

### 3. Place Admission Orders

- **Admission order set** — use the appropriate ICU admission order set in Epic
- **Diet, activity, nursing orders** — per patient acuity
- **Labs** — admission labs plus any disease-specific panels
- **Imaging** — CXR, echo, or other imaging as indicated
- **Telemetry/hemodynamic monitoring** — continuous monitoring orders
- **VTE prophylaxis** — unless contraindicated
- **Consults** — place formal consult orders in Epic and notify via Secure Chat (see [Epic Tips](../IT%20&%20Systems/onboarding-epic-tips.md))

### 4. Bedside Assessment & Procedures

- Complete your physical assessment
- Place any lines or access needed (arterial line, central line, dialysis catheter, sheath for MCS)
- Initiate MCS if ordered, with appropriate nursing communication orders

### 5. Documentation

- **Admission H&P** — complete on admission
- **Problem list** — update immediately for the new patient
- **Hospital course** — begin the narrative in story format
- **Add to patient list** — right-click > Assign Teams > "MH TMC Cardiology AHF (Treatment)"

## Discharge Workflow

### 1. Confirm Discharge Readiness

- Attending agrees patient is ready for discharge or transfer
- Pending results or studies are addressed or have follow-up plans

### 2. Place Discharge Orders

- **Medication reconciliation** — review and reconcile all medications
- **Patient instructions** — use the **`.hfdischarge`** dot phrase for HF-specific instructions
- **Follow-up appointments** — schedule or free-text if not already in the system
- **Home health** — if needed, place under Discharge > Ambulatory Home Health
- **Coumadin clinic referral** — if applicable, place "Ambulatory Referral to Anticoagulation — Warfarin Monitoring"

### 3. Complete the Discharge Summary

- Complete **on the day of discharge**
- Under Diagnoses, type **`.prob`** to pull in the problem list
- Hospital course should already be up to date from daily charting
- Verify follow-up appointments are documented

### 4. Handoff

- Communicate the discharge plan to nursing
- For transfers to the floor, provide a verbal handoff to the accepting team
- Patient automatically falls off the HFICU list after discharge
