## Code Status Changes

- **PCCM in-house attending** changes the code status order in Epic from Full Code or DNR to **Comfort Measures Only**
- Ensure all providers on the case are aware before the transition
- Notify the attending cardiologist and surgeon as appropriate per the [call tree](onboarding-call-tree.md)

## Comfort Care Orders

- Use the **Comfort Care order set** in Epic for symptom management (sedation, analgesia, anxiolytics)
- The comfort care order set does **not** include MCS-specific withdrawal orders — those must be entered separately

## MCS Withdrawal Process

### Nursing Communication Order

A **nursing communication order** must be placed to stop the device. This applies to:

- ECMO (VA and VV)
- Impella
- IABP
- LVAD / RVAD

### Teams to Notify

| Team | When Needed |
| --- | --- |
| All providers on the case | Always — everyone must be aware of the withdrawal plan |
| Perfusionist | ECMO, VAD cases |
| Circulatory support | Impella, IABP, device-specific needs |
| Respiratory therapy | Ventilator weaning or extubation |
| Chaplain | Family request or as clinically appropriate |
| Palliative care | Complex symptom management or family support |

### General Approach

- Coordinate timing with the family and care team
- Ensure adequate sedation and analgesia are in place **before** device withdrawal
- Device weaning vs. immediate discontinuation is determined by the clinical team on a case-by-case basis

## Documentation

- **Death note** — Fellows document the death note overnight
- **Event note** — APPs may document an event note if indicated
- Death pronouncement and time of death are documented per hospital policy by the fellow

## Organ & Tissue Donation

- **Life Gift** discussions and interventions are handled by **nursing**
- APPs do not need to initiate these conversations — nursing coordinates with the OPO
