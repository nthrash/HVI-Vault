## Vacation Request Guidelines

- As of March 2026, schedules will be released on a 2 month basis. 
	- January/Feb, March/April, Jun/July, etc.
- All requests **> 3 days** must be approved by **Amanda, Fidel, or Jessica**
- All requested days off for a schedule block must be submitted on the last day of the 3rd month back (Approximately 60 days).

| Schedule Block      | Requests Due By |
| ------------------- | --------------- |
| January & February  | October 31      |
| March & April       | December 31     |
| May & June          | February 28/29  |
| July & August       | April 30        |
| September & October | June 30         |
| November & December | August 31       |

- Vacation **> 7 days** must be submitted **4 months** in advance to guarantee approval
- **Max off at one time**: 2 on days, 1 on nights — keep this in mind when requesting
- **Weekends** should be fair and even; multiple weekends off in the same month may not be approved
- If a request is not approved, you can **switch shifts** with someone else to cover the days you want off
- Submit requests **before the deadline** listed in the schedule block table above to guarantee they are reviewed in time
- You can check the **status** of your request (pending, approved, denied) by returning to the Employee tab and viewing your submitted transactions
- If your request is denied, reach out to your charge nurse or scheduler to discuss alternatives such as shift swaps
- For requests **> 3 days**, ensure you have also communicated with **Amanda, Fidel, or Jessica** for approval

## How to Submit PTO in My Time and Schedule

> **Important:** This section covers how to apply your PTO hours to an **already approved** day off so you receive PTO pay instead of regular pay hours. All PTO requests must first be submitted and approved via email as described in the Vacation Request Guidelines above. Once approved, follow these steps to ensure your PTO bank is used.

PTO submissions are entered through the **API Healthcare (symplr) Time and Attendance** web application on the Memorial Hermann intranet.

### Step 1 — Log in and find the Home screen

Log in to **My Time and Schedule** through the Memorial Hermann intranet. You will see the Home screen with your menu options.

![Step 1 — Home screen](99%20-%20Meta/Assets/Images/ptoinput1.webp)

### Step 2 — Navigate to the Employee tab

Click the **Employee** tab in the top navigation bar. This will open your employee transaction view where you can see your current pay period and any existing transactions.

![Step 2 — Employee transaction view](99%20-%20Meta/Assets/Images/ptoinput2.webp)

### Step 3 — Open the Add Clocking form

Click the **Add Clocking** button (green) in the **Employee Favorites** section on the left sidebar. This opens the General transaction form where you will enter your PTO details.

![Step 3 — Add Clocking form](99%20-%20Meta/Assets/Images/ptoinput3.webp)

### Step 4 — Fill in your PTO details and save

Complete the required fields:

- **Date** — The date you are requesting off (e.g., `03/26/2026`)
- **Pay Code** — Enter `SPTOO` (self-scheduled PTO)
- **Time** — Your shift start time (e.g., `19:00`)
- **Hours/Units** — Number of hours for the day (e.g., `12.00`)
- **Requested Reason** — Brief description (e.g., `12 Hours PTO`)

Click **Save** when finished. Repeat for each day you are requesting off.

![Step 4 — Completed PTO form](99%20-%20Meta/Assets/Images/ptoinput4.webp)

## Memorial Hermann Holidays

| Holiday | Typical Date |
|---|---|
| New Year's Day | January 1 |
| Martin Luther King Jr. Day | 3rd Monday in January |
| Memorial Day | Last Monday in May |
| Independence Day | July 4 |
| Labor Day | 1st Monday in September |
| Thanksgiving Day | 4th Thursday in November |
| Christmas Day | December 25 |

### How Holidays Are Divided

- **Summer holidays** (MLK Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day) are grouped and divided among the team
- **Winter holidays** (Thanksgiving, New Year's Day, Christmas) are grouped and divided separately
- The goal is to distribute holiday coverage fairly so no one is working all of one group
