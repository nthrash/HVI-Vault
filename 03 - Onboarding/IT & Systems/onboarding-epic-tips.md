# Epic Tips

## Patient List Setup

- **Adding a patient**: Right-click the patient > **Assign Teams** > select **"MH TMC Cardiology AHF (Treatment)"**. Patients will automatically fall off the list after discharge.
- **Finding the list**: Go to Available Lists > MH TMC > Cardiology, then right-click and select **Add to My Group/Team Patients**
- **Customizing columns**: Right-click on My Group/Team > **Properties** > add columns and organize the layout to your preference

## Placing Orders

### Consults

All consults require a formal order — e.g., *"Inpatient Consult to Infectious Diseases"* or *"Inpatient Consult to Neurology"*. You can specify the provider (e.g., Dr. Levy) within the order. **Also send the team a message via Epic Secure Chat** to notify them of the consult.

### Cath Lab Scheduling

Place an order for **"Case Request Cath Lab"**. Fill in the date, attending, and procedure within the order.

### Coumadin Clinic Referral

Under discharge orders, place **"Ambulatory Referral to Anticoagulation — Warfarin Monitoring"**.

### Home Health Orders

Place under **Discharge** > **Ambulatory Home Health**. Note: there are known issues with infusion orders not populating correctly.

## Charting Responsibilities

### Problem List

All team members (day **and** night) must update and review the problem list regularly — especially for new admissions.

### Hospital Course

- Keep the hospital course updated **daily** in a **story format**
- This auto-populates into MD/APP notes and the discharge summary
- Include only **major events and procedures** (e.g., *"course complicated by RV failure"*) — not daily titration changes like diuretic adjustments
- Night team: ensure this is kept current for all HFICU patients

## Discharge Workflow

### Patient Instructions

Use the dot phrase **`.hfdischarge`** under Patient Instructions — it auto-populates HF-related discharge instructions (weight monitoring, etc.) and the clinic phone number.

### Discharge Summary

- Complete the discharge summary **on the day of discharge**
- Under Diagnoses, type **`.prob`** to pull in the problem list
- Follow-up appointments may not auto-populate — you may need to **free text** them if not already scheduled
- With the hospital course kept up to date, this should be straightforward

### Signing Notes

When signing notes, the charge screen will automatically appear — just **cancel** it (for MH employees).

## Useful Features

### Patient Timeline

Go to the **Summary** tab > **Overview** > **Patient Timeline** to see scheduled and past procedures at a glance.

### Customizing Your Layout

Click the **wrench icon** on the Summary/Overview tab > **Modify Layout** to reorder sections. This makes rounding easier.

### Viewing Attending Schedules

Go to the **Schedule** tab > click the small down arrow next to your name > **Create**. Name the schedule, then go to **Configuration** to add provider names and view their schedules.

### Inbox

Located at the **top right** of the Epic screen, and also **two tabs to the right** of the patient list. This is where verbal orders needing co-signature will appear. You will also get notifications in **Haiku**.

### Work Excuse Letters

Click the **down arrow** to access More Activities > **Communications**. Choose the note type, fill it out, and print.
