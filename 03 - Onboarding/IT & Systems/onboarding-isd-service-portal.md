title: ISD Service Portal
description: How to submit IT support requests through the ISD Service Portal
---

The ISD Service Portal is where you submit IT-related support requests — things like password resets, access issues, software installs, equipment problems, and general tech troubleshooting.

Access it through **OneSource** under the **ESSO Apps** or **Favorites** tab (look for "ISD Service Portal").

> **Tip:** Add this to your OneSource favorites — you will use it more than you think, especially during your first few weeks when access and permissions are still being set up.

---

## Common Requests

- **Account lockouts or password resets**
- **Epic access issues** — Missing roles, wrong department assignments
- **Badge access** — Door access not working for your unit
- **Printer setup** — Adding network printers to your workstation
- **Software requests** — Dragon Medical One, Centricity, specialty apps
- **VPN or remote access issues**
- **Hardware problems** — Broken monitors, keyboard/mouse replacements

---

## Tips

- Include your **employee ID**, **department**, and **location** when submitting a request — it speeds up routing
- For urgent issues (e.g., cannot access Epic during a shift), call the **ISD Help Desk** directly rather than submitting a portal ticket
- Check with your preceptor or charge nurse first — some common issues have quick local fixes
