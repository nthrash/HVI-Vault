## What Is OneSource?

OneSource is the Memorial Hermann employee intranet — the central hub for accessing clinical applications, internal tools, news, and system notifications. You can reach it from any hospital workstation or through the [Remote Access Portal](https://memorialhermann.workspaceair.com/).

> [OneSource](https://mhone.memorialhermann.org/home)

---

## Layout at a Glance

The top navigation bar includes tabs for **Physicians**, **About MH**, **Locations**, **Business Units**, **Patient Care**, **Resources**, **Education**, **Policies**, and **eHR**.

Below the nav bar you will find:

- **ESSO Apps / Corner Apps** — Quick-launch tiles for applications like Cerner EMR, Centricity Web, Corner Scheduling, Dragon Medical One, HIM Completion, HIM Retrieval, ISD Service Portal, OR Control Tracking, and Soarian
- **Favorites bar** — A customizable list of bookmarked applications (see below)
- **MH Now** — System news, events, and announcements
- **Notifications** — Alerts about outages, cyber status (CYBERCON level), and system-wide updates

---

## Setting Up Favorites

Click the **Favorites** tab at the bottom-left of the OneSource page, then use the **+** button to add applications. Starred items appear in your quick-access list.

### Suggested Favorites

These are commonly used by APPs across Memorial Hermann:

- **Maintenance Service Request** — Facilities work orders
- **Infor XM Expense Management** — Expense reports and reimbursement
- **TMC CE Travel & Certification Request** — CEU travel funding requests
- **Medical Staff Roster** — Provider lookup and privilege verification
- **MH Flu Registration** — Annual flu vaccination compliance
- **MHU Online** — Education modules and required training (see [MHU Online](../Clinical%20Access/onboarding-mhu-online.md))
- **My Time and Schedule** — PTO input, schedule view, benefit balances (see [Scheduling & PTO](../Unit%20Operations/onboarding-scheduling-pto.md))
- **HCPCS Code Checker Inpatient** — Verify procedure billing codes
- **Online Service Request (OSR)** — IT and general service tickets
- **Policy Tech** — Hospital policies and procedures (see [Hospital Policies](../Clinical%20Access/onboarding-hospital-policies.md))
- **UpToDate** — Clinical decision support (institutional access from hospital network)
- **VRS (Variance Reporting)** — Safety event reporting (see [Variance Reporting](../HR%20&%20Benefits/onboarding-variance-reporting.md))
- **Workday** — Pay, benefits, PTO, career info (see [Paychecks & Workday](../HR%20&%20Benefits/onboarding-paycheck-workday.md))
- **AMION** — Call and coverage schedules
