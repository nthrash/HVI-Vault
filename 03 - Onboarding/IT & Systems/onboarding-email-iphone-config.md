## Wi-Fi

Connect to **MH_GUEST** on your personal devices — no password required. This is the network used by staff, patients, and visitors throughout the campus.

---

## Hospital Email on Your iPhone

Make sure your **hospital email and calendar** are set up on your phone so you can stay up to date on all group communications and schedule changes.

## Adding Hospital Email (iPhone)

### Step 1 — Open Mail Settings

Open the **Settings** app and search for **Mail**, then tap **Mail Accounts**.

![](99%20-%20Meta/Assets/Images/Screenshot%202026-03-16%20at%2000.38.29.png)

### Step 2 — Add Account

Tap **Add Account** at the bottom of the Mail Accounts screen.

![](99%20-%20Meta/Assets/Images/Screenshot%202026-03-16%20at%2000.38.50.png)

### Step 3 — Choose Microsoft Exchange

Instead of typing in your email address as it states, click the "Choose from a list" button
From the provider list, select **Microsoft Exchange**.

![](99%20-%20Meta/Assets/Images/Screenshot%202026-03-16%20at%2000.40.19.png)

### Step 4 — Enter Account Details

Fill in the following fields:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Email** | Firstname.Lastname@memorialhermann.org |
| **Server** | owa.memorialhermann.org |
| **Domain** | memorial |
| **Username** | your A-number@mh.org (e.g., A1095561@mh.org) |
| **Password** | your hospital password |
| **Description** | MHHS |

Tap **Done** when finished.

![](99%20-%20Meta/Assets/Images/Screenshot%202026-03-16%20at%2000.41.38.png)

> Your A-number is your Memorial Hermann employee ID. If you don't know it, check your badge or ask your manager. Your A could also be at the end of your ID which should also work. 

### Step 5 — Verify

Once added, your hospital email and calendar should begin syncing automatically. You'll see **MHHS** listed under your Mail Accounts.
