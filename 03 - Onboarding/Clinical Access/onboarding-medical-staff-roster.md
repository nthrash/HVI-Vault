title: Medical Staff Roster
description: How to look up providers and verify clinical privileges across MHHS
---

The Medical Staff Roster is a tool to look up all providers (including fellows and residents) that operate within the Memorial Hermann Health System. You can view contact information, office locations, and hospital privileges.

Access it through OneSource under the **Favorites** tab or by searching "Medical Staff Roster" in the search bar.

---

## Looking Up a Provider

1. Type the **provider's last name** (or even just the first few letters) in the search field
2. Click **Submit**
3. A list of matching providers will appear — each row shows the provider name, medical staff number, and the **facility codes** where they have privileges (e.g., TMC, MHE, MHMD)
4. Click on a **facility code** to see that provider's details at that location

The detail page shows:

- **Facility Privileges** — Provider name, facility, current status, and faculty appointment
- **Physician Details** — Primary and additional specialties, office name, address, phone, fax, pager, and NPI number

---

## Viewing Clinical Privileges (MSOW / VerityStream)

To see exactly what procedures a provider is privileged to perform:

1. From the provider detail page, click **View Privileges** at the bottom
2. This opens the **MSOW (VerityStream)** portal in a new tab
3. Select **Find Facility** — type "H" and choose **Memorial Hermann Texas Medical Center** (or the appropriate facility)
4. In the **Find Practitioner** field, retype the provider's last name
5. Hit **Enter**
6. The privileges page will display the provider's full privilege list organized by category

### What You Will See

Privileges are broken out by category levels. For example, for a Cardiology attending:

- **Age-Specific Privileges** — Patient population the provider is credentialed to treat
- **Category I** — Uncomplicated medical conditions without significant threat
- **Category II** — Acute problems of moderate or critical severity, plus general procedures (arterial cannulation, central lines, intubation, thoracentesis, etc.)
- **Category III** — Specialty procedures (non-invasive cardiology, cardiovascular ultrasound, etc.)

For an APP (Nurse Practitioner), you will see:

- **Category I: Core Privileges** — Prescriptive authority, daily rounding, documentation, diagnostic ordering, discharge, determination of death, etc.
- **Category II: Specialty Privileges** — Specific procedures with minimum numbers for initial privileging (e.g., arterial line insertion: 15, central line insertion ultrasound-guided: 20, intubation: 15, chest tube removal: 10, etc.)

---

## Why This Matters

Before asking a colleague to perform a procedure or co-sign an order at a specific facility, you can verify they actually hold privileges there. This is especially useful when working with covering providers, moonlighters, or consulting teams you are not familiar with.
