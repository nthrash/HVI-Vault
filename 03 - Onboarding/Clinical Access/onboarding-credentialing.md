Once credentialed, you can verify your status and manage your credentials through the **eCredentialing Portal**:

> [eCredentialing Portal](https://psv.memorialhermann.org/PractitionerPortal/Login.aspx)

Log in with your Memorial Hermann credentials.

## Paying and Submitting Your Application

Once you have submitted all required documents and forms, follow these steps to complete payment and final submission:

1. Disable your popup blocker if necessary.
2. Log back into the portal.
3. Click on the **"View Your Responses"** hyperlink next to Documents.
4. Click on the **"Review, Pay, and Submit"** tab, then click the blue **Continue** button.
5. **To pay** — Click the blue payment button. A new window will open showing your invoice and the amount owed. You can pay by **eCheck** or **credit card**. Close that window after payment to return to your application. Your application is **not yet submitted** at this point.
6. **If payment is pending** — Click the blue **Submit** button. A new window will ask for your date of birth and Social Security number. Enter your date of birth in `MM/DD/YYYY` format (with slashes) and your SSN in `###-##-####` format (with dashes). Your application is now submitted.

> **Having trouble submitting?** Contact the credentialing help desk:
> - Email: credentialing.helpdesk@memorialhermann.org
> - Phone: 713-338-6464 (select option 3, then option 1)
