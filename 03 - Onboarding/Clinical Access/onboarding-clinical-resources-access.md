## Remote Access Portal

Access hospital applications from home through the **Workspace ONE** portal:

> [memorialhermann.workspaceair.com](https://memorialhermann.workspaceair.com/)

From here you can launch OneSource, MHU Online, and other hospital apps without being on campus. For a full virtual desktop experience (file access, Epic Hyperspace, etc.), use:

> [VDI Desktop — vdi.memorialhermann.org](https://vdi.memorialhermann.org/portal/webclient/#/desktop)

## UpToDate

Memorial Hermann provides institutional access to **UpToDate**, the clinical decision support resource.

- **On campus:** Access UpToDate from any hospital workstation or while connected to the hospital network — no login required. You can also find it in your OneSource Favorites (see [OneSource Overview](../IT%20&%20Systems/onboarding-onesource-overview.md)).
- **Off campus:** To activate mobile/home access, first visit UpToDate from a hospital workstation while on the network. This registers your institutional affiliation. After that, you can log in from anywhere using your personal UpToDate account for a limited period before needing to re-verify on campus.
- **Mobile app:** Download the UpToDate app (iOS/Android), log in with your personal account, and your institutional access will carry over as long as you periodically re-verify on the hospital network.

## Amion (Call Schedules)

**Amion** is used to view on-call and coverage schedules for attending physicians, fellows, and APPs.

- Add it to your **OneSource Favorites** for quick access from any workstation
- Ask your preceptor or scheduler for the group password if one is required for your unit's schedule
- You can also access Amion directly at [amion.com](https://www.amion.com) — search for Memorial Hermann or your specific department
