# Cafeteria & Food Options

Memorial Hermann-Texas Medical Center offers a variety of food options for patients and visitors.

## Café Hermann
Full-service cafeteria menu that includes entrées, pizza, grill and salad bar.
- **Location:** Robertson Pavilion, 1st floor
- **Breakfast:** 6:00 - 10:00 a.m., Monday through Friday
- **Lunch:** 11:00 a.m. - 2:00 p.m., Monday through Friday

## Arboretum Cafe at the Sarofim Pavilion
The largest cafeteria at Memorial Hermann-Texas Medical Center. Its expanded menu includes food offerings such as sushi and more vegan and vegetarian options.
- **Location:** Sarofim Pavilion, 2nd floor
- **Hours:** 6:00 a.m. - 2:00 a.m., Daily
- **Closed:** 10:00 - 11:00 a.m. and 4:00 - 5:00 p.m.

## Other Food Vendors

### Red's Cafe
Soup, sandwiches, salads, snacks, coffee, fresh made-to-order paninis and flatbreads, drinks and ice cream.
- **Location:** Cullen Pavilion, 2nd floor (near Skybridge and Larry D. Johnson Heart & Vascular Institute)
- **Hours:** Open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week

### Bistro Sixty411
Sandwiches, salads, pastries, desserts and gourmet coffee including cappuccino and espresso.
- **Location:** Cullen Pavilion, 2nd floor (near escalators)
- **Hours:** 6:00 a.m. - 2:00 p.m., Monday through Friday

### Chick-Fil-A
Serves limited menu.
- **Location:** University of Texas Professional Building (UTPB) at 6410 Fannin St
- **Hours:** 7:30 a.m. - 2:30 p.m., Monday through Friday

### Starbucks
- **Location:** Memorial Hermann Medical Plaza, 1st floor
- **Hours:** 5:00 a.m. - 7:00 p.m., Monday through Friday; 5:30 a.m. - 4:00 p.m., Saturday
