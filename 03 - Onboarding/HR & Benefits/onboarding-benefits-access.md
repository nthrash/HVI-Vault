## New Hire Checklist

The following steps must be completed as part of your onboarding with Memorial Hermann. For full details, see the [New Hire Checklist](https://yourmhbenefits.com/new-hires/new-hire-checklist/).

### 1. Background Check & Pre-Employment Health Screening

- Both must be completed simultaneously to determine employment eligibility
- Resources: Background Check documentation, Employee Health – New Hire Service Center, LabCorp options

### 2. Required Licenses & Certifications

- Copies of any required licenses and certifications must be provided **before** your first day can be scheduled

### 3. Communication Checkpoint

- Once background check and health screening are cleared, New Hire Services will contact you to confirm eligibility to proceed

### 4. Schedule First Day

- New Hire Services coordinates with you and your supervisor to set a start date

### 5. Workday System Access

- Login information is provided within **24 hours** of your confirmed start date

### 6. Form I-9 Completion

- Section 1: Completed through the Guardian/LawLogix platform
- Remote hires: Authorize a representative for Section 2
- Must be completed within **3 business days** of your start date

### 7. New Employee Orientation

- Dates and location are provided after scheduling your first day
- Visit the [First Day and Orientation](https://yourmhbenefits.com/new-hires/) page for details

### 8. Benefits Enrollment

- You have **30 calendar days** from your date of hire to enroll in benefits
- Enrollment is done through **Workday**

### 9. Retirement Plan Enrollment

- Memorial Hermann offers a Fidelity Investments retirement plan with a **6% employer match**

### 10. Payroll Setup

- Direct deposit must be entered by **10:00 AM Monday** before your first payroll cycle
- If not set up in time, you will default to an ePay card

### 11. Text Messaging Sign-Up (Optional)

- Enroll to receive benefits information and reminders via text

### 12. Culture Expo Attendance

- Final step that completes the new hire checklist
