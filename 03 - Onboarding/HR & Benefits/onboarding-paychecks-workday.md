## Viewing Pay Stubs

1. Go to **Workday**
2. Click the **menu** at the top left
3. Click **Pay**
4. Click **Paychecks Stubs & eW-2**
5. Log in if prompted
6. Select the date for the check you want to view

## Viewing W-2s

Your W-2 forms from previous years are also available in the same location:

1. Follow the steps above to navigate to **Paychecks Stubs & eW-2**
2. Select the tax year you want to view
