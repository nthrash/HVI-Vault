title: Variance Reporting

---

A variance report documents any unexpected event, near-miss, or safety concern. Submitting a variance is not punitive — it helps the organization identify patterns and improve patient and staff safety.

---

## How to Access VRS

1. Go to the **MHOne** intranet page from the hospital network or remote access portal
2. In the **Web Applications** section, click the **Business** tab
3. Scroll down (or search) for **VRS (Variance Reporting)** and click it
4. You will be presented with a login screen — you can log in with your credentials or click **Submit Anonymously**

---

## Submitting a Variance Report

Once you log in, you will see an **Icon Wall** with category options. Select the one that best fits the event you are reporting:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Blood Product** | Transfusion reactions, wrong blood product administered |
| **Care Management** | Delays in care, discharge issues, treatment plan concerns |
| **Equipment / Medical Devices / Supplies** | Malfunctioning equipment, missing supplies |
| **Fall (Patient)** | Any patient fall, witnessed or unwitnessed |
| **Lab Test / Specimen** | Mislabeled specimens, lost samples, incorrect test orders |
| **Medication / Fluid** | Wrong dose, wrong medication, wrong route, missed dose |
| **Non-Patient (Visitor / Non-MHHS Employee)** | Incidents involving visitors or non-employees |
| **Professional Conduct** | Behavioral concerns, policy violations |
| **Safety Security Environment** | Hazards, security threats, environmental concerns |
| **Skin Tissue** | Pressure injuries, skin tears, wound-related events |
| **Workplace Violence** | Verbal or physical aggression toward staff |
| **QPSIC DEPT Use Only: Quality Review** | Reserved for Quality department use |
| **Regulatory Visit** | Reserved for documenting regulatory agency visits |

After selecting the appropriate category:

1. Complete all **required fields** as indicated on the form
2. Provide as much detail as possible — include the date, time, location, and a factual description of the event
3. **Submit** the report
4. **Write down or take a picture of the reference report code** — this is your confirmation number for tracking

---

## Tips

- Report as close to the time of the event as possible while details are fresh
- Stick to objective facts — avoid assigning blame or speculation
- Anonymous submissions are accepted, but logging in allows follow-up if needed
- If you are unsure which category to select, choose the closest match — the Quality team will reroute if necessary
