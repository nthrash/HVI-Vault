Keeping your licenses and certifications current is your responsibility. If anything lapses, you cannot practice or prescribe — and the hospital will pull your privileges until it's resolved. Set calendar reminders well in advance of each expiration.

---

## Licenses & Certifications to Track

| License / Certification | Renewal Cycle | Governing Body | CE Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Texas RN License** | Every 2 years | Texas Board of Nursing (BON) | 20 contact hours per renewal period |
| **Texas APRN License** | Every 2 years | Texas BON (renews with or separately from RN) | Included in RN CE requirements |
| **DEA Registration** | Every 3 years | U.S. Drug Enforcement Administration | None (fee-based renewal) |
| **Texas CSR** (Controlled Substance Registration) | Every 1 year | Texas DPS | None (fee-based renewal) |
| **National Certification** (ANCC, AANP, or other certifying body) | Varies by certifying body (typically every 5 years) | ANCC, AANP, etc. | Varies — check your certifying body's requirements |
| **BLS** | Every 2 years | American Heart Association | Renewal course |
| **ACLS** | Every 2 years | American Heart Association | Renewal course |
| **Hospital Credentialing** | Every 2 years (typical) | Memorial Hermann via [eCredentialing Portal](https://psv.memorialhermann.org/PractitionerPortal/Login.aspx) | Completed by medical staff office — you provide updated documents |

---

## When to Start Your Renewal

Most licensing boards allow renewal **60–90 days** before expiration. Don't wait until the last minute — processing delays can leave you unable to work.

| Item | Recommended Lead Time |
|---|---|
| Texas RN / APRN License | 60 days before expiration |
| DEA Registration | 90 days before expiration |
| Texas CSR | 60 days before expiration |
| National Certification | 90+ days (some require portfolio submission) |
| BLS / ACLS | Schedule your renewal course 1–2 months ahead |

## Tips for Staying Current

- **Keep a personal tracking spreadsheet** with expiration dates for every license and certification
- **Set two reminders** for each: one at 90 days out and one at 60 days out
- **Store digital copies** of all current licenses and certifications in a personal folder — you'll need them for credentialing, moonlighting applications, and insurance
- **Report renewals to the hospital** — after renewing any license, submit your updated documentation to the medical staff office so your credentialing file stays current
- **Don't let CE hours pile up** — spread them across the renewal period instead of cramming at the end
