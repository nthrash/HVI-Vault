## Overview

Memorial Hermann uses **Infor XM Expense Management** to process expense reimbursements, including licensing costs. You will submit an Expense Request through the MHOne intranet.

---

## How to Submit a Reimbursement

### 1. Access MHOne Intranet

- Go to [MHOne Intranet](https://mhone.memorialhermann.org/home)

### 2. Open Infor XM Expense Management

- In the **Web Applications** area, click the **Infor XM Expense Management** icon (red icon with the word "Infor")

### 3. Create an Expense Request

1. Click **Create New** (a dropdown menu will appear)
2. Click **Expense Request**
3. Select the appropriate **purpose** for the expense request
4. Give the request a **title** (e.g., "RN License Renewal 2026")
