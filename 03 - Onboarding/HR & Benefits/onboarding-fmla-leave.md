## Memorial Hermann Leave Management Center (MHLMC)

FMLA and disability leave claims are administered by **Sedgwick**. You can file a new claim, check on an existing claim, or report a return-to-work date by phone or online.

| Resource | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Phone (IVR)** | 855-547-3307 — select 2 from the Main Menu (available 24/7) |
| **Online** | mySedgwick via Workday (Home > Benefits Application) or [mySedgwick.com/memorialhermann](https://mySedgwick.com/memorialhermann) |

---

## Filing a New Claim

Call the Sedgwick IVR at **855-547-3307** and say **"New Claim."** After verifying your identity you will be transferred to a Service Center Representative.

Have the following ready:

- **9-digit Employee ID number**
- **Date of birth**

You can also file online through mySedgwick via Workday.

---

## Checking an Existing Claim

Call the IVR and say **"Existing Claim."** Allow **one business day** after filing before the automated system will have your claim information.

Once authenticated, you can hear:

- Status of the most current disability claim
- Approved benefit period
- Last medical update received and when the next medical report is due
- Return-to-work date
- Status of concurrent leave (if applicable)
- Approved leave start and end dates
- Denied leave status and reason
- Other open claims or claims closed within the last six months

---

## Reporting a Return to Work

Call the IVR and say **"Return to Work"** to report your actual or estimated return-to-work date.

---

## Getting Contact Information

Call the IVR and say **"Contact Information,"** then specify whether you need the **mailing address** or the **fax number**.

---

## Need Help?

At any point during the IVR call, say **"Agent"** or press **0** to speak with a Service Center Representative.
