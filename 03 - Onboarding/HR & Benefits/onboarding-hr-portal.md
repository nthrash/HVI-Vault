## HR Portal

**[Memorial Hermann HR Portal](https://memorialhermann.portal.dovetailnow.com)**

> Accessible from the hospital network or [remote access portal](https://memorialhermann.workspaceair.com/).

The HR Portal is your one-stop hub for employment-related information. The top navigation bar includes the following sections, each with drop-down menus for related topics:

### My Money

- Compensation details
- Payroll information overview
- Retirement plan information
- **Note:** Actual pay stubs and W-2s are accessed through **Workday**, not the HR Portal (see [Paychecks & Workday](onboarding-paychecks-workday.md))

### My Benefits

- View all benefit details (medical, dental, vision, etc.)
- **Note:** Reviewing benefit information is done here, but benefit *enrollment and changes* are done through **Workday**

### My Moments that Matter

- Work events (promotions, transfers, etc.)
- Life events (marriage, new child, etc.)

### My Career

- Career development resources
- Internal job opportunities

### My Time Off

- PTO balances and policies
- Leave of absence information

### My Workday

- Quick links to Workday tasks and resources

### Ask a Question

- Submit HR questions directly through the portal
